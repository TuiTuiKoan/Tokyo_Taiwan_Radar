01 ・ 3 Facts ②

Words Are Not Neutral or Objective

Interfaces remove ambiguity with menus; conversation hands the ambiguity to the system untouched. A simple description carries stereotypes and unclear rules, common sense, bias, subjectivity and past experience.

“What could be considered before asking? What needs to be further verified?” becomes a design question.
